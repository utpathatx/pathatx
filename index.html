One trail, from kindergarten to college

The help changes as students grow. The goal stays the same: every student finishes high school knowing exactly how to get to college.

  1. Grades K–5

    Elementary school

    Weekly tutoring that builds strong readers and confident math students, with the same UT volunteer every week.

    • Reading
    • Math practice
    • Homework help
  2. Grades 6–8

    Middle school

    Mentoring and study habits for the years when grades start to count, plus a first real look at what college is.

    • Study skills
    • Mentoring
    • Goal setting
  3. Grades 9–12

    High school

    The steps nobody explains, broken down one at a time: tests, essays, financial aid and applications.

    • SAT and ACT basics
    • Essay feedback
    • FAFSA help
    • Applications
  4. The goal

    College

    Campus visits to UT so college feels like a real place, and a mentor who has been through the process.

    • UT campus visits
    • College mentors

How volunteering works

Any UT student can join. You don't need teaching experience, just an hour or two a week and the patience to show up for the same students all semester.

  1. Apply

    Fill out the short form below and tell us which grades and subjects you'd like to work with.

  2. Get cleared

    Complete a background check and youth-protection training before your first session.

  3. Get matched

    We place you with a partner school or program that fits your class schedule.

  4. Show up weekly

    Same students, same time, every week. That consistency is what makes the difference.

We work alongside Austin schools and nonprofits and follow their volunteer screening, along with UT's Youth Protection Program rules for any events on campus.

Questions

Something else on your mind? Email utpathatx@gmail.com.

Do I need tutoring or teaching experience?

No. We train every volunteer before their first session. Patience and showing up every week matter more than a résumé.

What's the time commitment?

One weekly session with your students, plus a training at the start of the semester. You choose times that fit your class schedule.

Do I need a car?

No. Tell us how you get around in your application and we'll match you with a school you can reach.

Is PATH only for high school students?

No. We work with elementary, middle and high school students. College prep is for high schoolers; tutoring and mentoring are for every grade.

Are volunteers background-checked?

Yes. Everyone who works with students completes the screening and training our partner schools require before their first session.

Which schools do you work with?

We're building our first partnerships now. If you're a counselor, teacher or parent, send us a request below.
